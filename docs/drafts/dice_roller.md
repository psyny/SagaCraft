We will have s stage set that is the dice roller.

The options of dices there are selectable by the saga template defualts, and changed by admin as set parameters.
Options are:

rolls types, example:
-dice: d20/d10/d12/etc. result type: total sum, greater of them, smaller of them, sum of dices of values above/below X.
so, a list of possible rolls and how to interpret their result.

On live stage, we will have the dice tray. Where we can click in the roll types, this will add the dice to the tray, and then we can click roll.
The tray is just the dices being added to the bottom of the set, in row, side by side, and everyone can see them being added.

When roll is clicked, the dices are rolled on the set. They colide with set boundaries and each other. You will need to add some rolling physics here, but nothing realist or complex, its just to give the impression they are flying and rolling.

When the dies are on the tray, the DM can have the option, by clicking on them to select the number that is going to land. Of course players wont see the DM selecting the number there. This is to give DM some control but still give the players the thrill of the roll.

as user/player options, users have a die style and player color. thise are to be taken into consideration.

Dices are 3d pieces, i dont know how you are going to handle this. but do.

They have textures, shaders, etc.

And I want you to give the players option of many types of dices:

-Minimalist:  default option. A bit glossy, a bit rounded edges. Main color is the player color, the number color is a color with good contrast with player color (use color heory here, you will need color theory tools in our app for many things). The number color pick is based on player color, and pick a complimentary color of that, something that is very visible.

-Ember: die is black with low gloss, with small cracks glowing the player color. The numbers glow the player color.

-Metal: die is very reflective like. A bit of a brushed texture (yes, normal maps and such), of player color. The numbers are white or black, depending to what makes more contrast with player color.

-Raw Wood: the die is light beige, raw wood color, wood texture, and the numbers are bright (not glowing) player color.

-polished wood....

-noble: some fancy looking texture. Pick how to use player colors here as you want.

-Elven, dwarven, orcish, nature... be reative, play with dice shapes when possible. but always use player color as the main differentiator of colors.
